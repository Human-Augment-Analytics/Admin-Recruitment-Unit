# Reminder Email Procedure

## Intended Audience
Recruitment leads responsible for following up with applicants who have pending actions.

---

## Purpose
To remind applicants of required actions and deadlines in a clear and concise way.

---

## Procedure

1. Copy the reminder email template below.
2. Replace all bracketed fields with applicant-specific information.
3. Confirm:
   - Required action is clearly stated
   - Deadline is included
4. Send the email to the applicant.

---

## Email Template

Subject: Reminder: HAAG Application Action Required

Dear [Applicant Name],

This is a reminder to complete the following required action(s):
- [Action]

Please complete this by [Deadline].

If you have already completed this, please disregard this message.

Best regards,  
[Your Name]  
HAAG Recruitment Team

---

## Procedure Evaluation

**Clarity of Audience**  
The procedure is intended for recruitment leads managing follow-up communication.

**Appropriate Level of Detail**  
The steps are concise and sufficient for completing the task.

**Reproducibility**  
The process can be repeated consistently using the provided template.

**Realism of Effort**  
The procedure reduces workload by eliminating the need to draft reminders manually.

**Integration & Enforcement**  
Templates can be stored centrally and used as a standard reference, supporting consistent communication practices.
