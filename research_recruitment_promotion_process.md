# Research Recruitment Promotion Process

This document outlines the procedure for promoting HAAG research opportunities in order to recruit new researchers and expand participation in interdisciplinary research projects. The process integrates research promotion strategies and stakeholder engagement practices to maximize visibility and ensure that opportunities reach qualified candidates.

---

# Recruitment Promotion Workflow

## 1. Identify Recruitment Needs

Before promoting research opportunities, determine which projects require recruitment.

* Identify projects that require additional researchers
* Consult project leads and faculty advisors to confirm staffing needs
* Determine the number of students needed for each research team
* Document required skills, expected time commitment, and project goals

Clearly identifying recruitment needs ensures that promotional efforts target the appropriate audience.

---

## 2. Identify Stakeholders Who Can Support Recruitment

Recruitment is strengthened by engaging relevant stakeholders within and outside the research group.

* Identify stakeholders such as faculty advisors, computational advisors, alumni, and current researchers
* Determine which stakeholders can promote opportunities within their networks
* Coordinate messaging so stakeholders share consistent information about the research opportunities
* Encourage stakeholders to recommend strong candidates

Engaging stakeholders increases the reach of recruitment efforts and helps connect research opportunities with qualified candidates.

---

## 3. Develop Recruitment Messaging

Create a clear description of each research opportunity to attract potential applicants.

Each recruitment message should include:

* A concise overview of the research project
* The research problem and why it matters
* Expected responsibilities for student researchers
* Skills or background that would be beneficial
* Application instructions and deadlines

Messaging should be accessible to students from multiple disciplines and avoid unnecessary technical jargon.

---

## 4. Prepare Recruitment Materials

Gather materials that support promotion of research opportunities.

Examples include:

* Short project descriptions suitable for announcements
* Visual materials such as diagrams or project summaries
* Quotes or testimonials from current researchers
* Links to publications, project repositories, or research websites

These materials help prospective applicants understand the scope and impact of the research.

---

## 5. Promote Research Opportunities

Distribute recruitment announcements through multiple channels to reach a broad audience.

Common promotion channels include:

* OMSCS community announcements (such as Reddit posts)
* HAAG website project listings
* HAAG seminars or information sessions
* Research showcases and presentations
* Social media, newsletters, or blogs

Using multiple channels increases visibility and ensures research opportunities reach students with diverse interests.

---

## 6. Engage Interested Applicants

Once promotion begins, provide opportunities for interested students to learn more about the research.

* Host information sessions or Q&A discussions
* Respond to applicant questions about projects or expectations
* Connect potential applicants with project leads or current researchers
* Provide guidance on how to prepare a strong application

This step helps applicants understand the research environment and encourages motivated candidates to apply.

---

## 7. Maintain Communication With Stakeholders

Throughout the recruitment process, keep stakeholders informed about progress.

* Provide updates on the number of applicants received
* Notify project leads about potential candidates
* Coordinate with advisors regarding candidate evaluation
* Share recruitment outcomes with stakeholders

Regular communication helps ensure that recruitment aligns with project needs and stakeholder expectations.

---

## 8. Track Recruitment Outcomes

Maintain records of recruitment efforts to evaluate effectiveness.

* Track which promotional channels generated applications
* Record the number of applicants per project
* Document applicant qualifications and project placements
* Identify challenges encountered during recruitment

Tracking recruitment outcomes helps improve future recruitment cycles.

---

## 9. Reflect and Improve Recruitment Strategy

After the recruitment process concludes, evaluate the effectiveness of the strategy.

* Identify which promotion methods produced the strongest applicants
* Gather feedback from project leads and new researchers
* Assess whether recruitment met project staffing needs
* Adjust recruitment messaging or promotion channels for future cycles

Continuous improvement ensures that research recruitment efforts remain effective and sustainable.
