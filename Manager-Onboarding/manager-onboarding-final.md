# Management & Leadership Final

_By: Alexa Accuardi_

### Initiative Scope

The purpose of this initiative is to improve the process of onboarding for managers by improving how information related to active enrollments within HAAG is organized. The deliverable will be a procedure that outlines the process for getting organized at the start of each semester and a newly formatted master excel spreadsheet.

### Evidence

One of HAAG's missions is "Leadership & Project Management Development". The idea to focus on onboarding for the leadership and management students originally stemmed from challenges I had understanding the structure of HAAG and the course. Before developing the procedure, I interviewed three other managers on Microsoft Teams to better understand what resources they used and what challenges they had at the beginning of the term. The questions I used in those interviews can be found [here](https://github.com/Human-Augment-Analytics/Admin-Recruitment-Unit/blob/main/Manager-Onboarding/interview-questions.md).

From this research four themes related to the mangers' experiences emerged.

1. **HAAG Organization.** Many reported being unfamiliar with HAAG prior to enrolling in this course. This created confusion related to the manager's role as well as other components of HAAG.
2. **Course Structure and Communication.** Managers desired more structure for the course particularly around the syllabus, assignment instructions, and assignment submission.
3. **Accurate Enrollment Information.** Students reported inaccurate data in the HAAG master spreadsheet and found the document difficult to navigate.
4. **Weekly Meeting Structure.** Format of the weekly meetings felt disorganized and made it difficult to glean information.

### Procedures

- [Manager Onboarding Procedure](https://github.com/Human-Augment-Analytics/Admin-Recruitment-Unit/blob/main/Manager-Onboarding/manager-onboarding-procedure.md). The purpose of this procedure is to outline information about HAAG and the for managers to help them have a more seamless onboarding process at the beginning of the semester.
- [Manager Onboarding PowerPoint](https://github.com/Human-Augment-Analytics/Admin-Recruitment-Unit/blob/main/Manager-Onboarding/manager-onboarding-procedure.pptx). The puprose of this accompanying document is to provide a resource for the leadership and management instructor to introduce the students to HAAG and the course during the first live session. It will also serve as a nice artifact that students can refer back to (especially for those that are unable to attend live).
- [Improved Enrollment Spreadsheet](https://github.com/Human-Augment-Analytics/Admin-Recruitment-Unit/blob/main/Manager-Onboarding/HAAG-master-spreadsheet.xlsx). The purpose of this accompanying document is to provide an easier mechanism for gathering data and enrollments for each term. While the data problem is out of scope for this project, the hope is that this improved layout makes it easier to audit and provide information from Canvas enrollment exports, for example.
