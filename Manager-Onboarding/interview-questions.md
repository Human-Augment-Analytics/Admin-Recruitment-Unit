# Interview Questions

This document contains the proposed interview questions for the manager onboarding initiative.

_By: Alexa Accuardi_

---

### Introduction

- How many classes have you taken toward your OMS degree?
- What knowledge of HAAG did you have prior to this semester?
- Can you walk me through how you got started at the beginning of the semester?
- What resources did you rely on?

### Pain Points

- What parts of the onboarding process felt confusing or unclear?
- Were there moments where you didn’t know what to do next?
- Did you experience any challenges with the master spreadsheet?

### Manager Experience

- How long did it take you to feel comfortable in your role?
- If you could change one thing about the onboarding process, what would it be?
- What would make the spreadsheet easier to use?

### Closing

- Is there anything else you'd like to share about your experience onboarding as a manager this semester?
