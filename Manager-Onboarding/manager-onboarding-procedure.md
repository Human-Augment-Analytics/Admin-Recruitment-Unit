# Manager Onboarding Procedure

_By: Alexa Accuardi_

The goal of this procedure is to help OMS students from the Management & Leadership course become familiar with HAAG.

## Introduction to HAAG

The Human-Augmented Analytics Group (HAAG) is an experimental virtual laboratory for applied data science collaboration. Founded by Director Breanna Shi in May 2024 as a 4th-year Bioinformatics PhD student with just 15 participants, HAAG has rapidly grown to host 125 researchers across masters, PhD, post-doctoral, and faculty levels.

HAAG is unique in centering masters students as the primary researchers in interdisciplinary projects. Students from the OMSCS program get the opportunity to be mentored by both their faculty affiliate in an applied field and their computational advisor, a doctoral or post-doctoral researcher trained in computer science or applied mathematics field of study.

### HAAG's Mission

- **Applied Research Education.** Training the next generation of data scientists through hands-on interdisciplinary projects that earn academic credit and research distinctions.
- **Leadership & Project Management Development.** Preparing future faculty, PhD researchers, and MBA students with experience-based training in managing complex collaborative research goals.
- **Cross-Disciplinary Collaboration.** Creating sustainable partnerships between computational and domain experts through structured frameworks.
- **Research Acceleration.** Expanding faculty research capacity through virtual laboratory partnerships that minimize advisor fatigue while maximizing research outcomes.

### HAAG Roles

| Role                   | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Faculty                | Interdisciplinary faculty with application-based expertise in need of computational talent whose role is to advise on all aspects of the project related to the application.                                                               |
| Computational Advisors | Volunteer-based PhD or Post-doctoral researchers in computational or quantitative fields that serve as the primary mentors for the researchers.                                                                                            |
| Researchers            | Computational students enrolled in a credit-based research course and assigned to a project. Their primary role is to perform the requested computation and documentation tasks to satisfy a shared tool generation & publication outcome. |
| Managers               | Computational students enrolled in a credit-based management and leadership course and assigned to a project. Their primary role is to implement and track the success of management techniques in the research environment.               |

Additional roles such as admins and TAs facilitate program management and operations.

## Course Overview

### Course Components

| Component     | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Live Sessions | This class meets weekly synchronously. This time is used for course announcements and live problem-solving and collaboration with other managers related to your operations and initiatives. Students unable to attend the live session will need to watch the recording and complete a short write-up. |
| Operations    | The operations portion of this course captures the work you do with your research team. This includes reviewing your researchers’ weekly reports and surfacing any issues that arise. We encourage you to select a team where you can attend their biweekly meetings.                                   |
| Initiative    | The initiative component allows students to define and improve processes within HAAG such as high-performance computing, recruitment, collaboration, engagement, faculty relations, and tracking. This portion of the course will be introduced in more detail later in the term.                       |

### Weekly Responsibilities

To give you a rough idea of what a typical week this semester will look like, here is a quick overview. Your instructor and TAs will go into more detail as the semester progresses.

- **Operations Tracking.** Each Friday, every researcher on your team will submit a weekly report. You will then take those reports and consolidate them into an operations spreadsheet.
- **Attend Live Session.** If you are available, attend the live class session. This time is used to check-in with other managers and announce upcoming assignments. If you are unable to attend, watch the recording and submit a reflection within 48 hours. Your reflection should follow this structure:

  _Create a "teaching guide" with bullet points, examples, and practice problems
  that would use to explains 3 concepts covered in this class. You may validate
  these concept with external resources, but you must provide the source._

- **Attend Research Meetings.** Most research teams meet on a biweekly cadence. We recommend selecting a research team whose meeting time aligns with your schedule to get the most out of the course.
