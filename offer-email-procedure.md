# Offer Email Procedure

## Intended Audience
Recruitment leads responsible for sending offer emails to selected applicants.

---

## Purpose
To ensure all offer emails clearly communicate acceptance, required actions, deadlines, and next steps in a consistent format.

---

## Procedure

1. Copy the offer email template below.
2. Replace all bracketed fields with applicant-specific information.
3. Confirm:
   - The acceptance decision is stated in the first paragraph
   - Required actions are clearly listed
   - A deadline is included
4. Send the email to the applicant.
5. (Optional) Record the email in recruitment tracking.

---

## Email Template

Subject: HAAG Application Result

Dear [Applicant Name],

We are pleased to inform you that you have been selected to join HAAG.

To accept this offer, please complete the following steps:
- [Action 1]
- [Action 2]

Please complete these steps by [Deadline].

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out.

Best regards,  
[Your Name]  
HAAG Recruitment Team

---

## Procedure Evaluation

**Clarity of Audience**  
The procedure is written specifically for recruitment leads responsible for applicant communication.

**Appropriate Level of Detail**  
The procedure includes only necessary steps (copy, edit, send), which matches the needs of users who are familiar with email communication.

**Reproducibility**  
All required steps and a complete template are provided, allowing the process to be followed without additional context.

**Realism of Effort**  
The procedure minimizes effort by using a pre-written template, requiring only minor edits.

**Integration & Enforcement**  
The template is intended to be stored in a shared repository and used as the default for sending offer emails. Consistency is encouraged through ease of use rather than strict enforcement.
