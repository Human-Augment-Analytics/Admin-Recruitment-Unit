# HAAG Open House / Project Showcase (Main Event) Procedure
To be planned and executed by one or two managers or admins.

## Assemble Project Representative Group
1. Create **#open-house** public slack channel if one does not already exist.
2. Message managers for each project and ask them to join the channel or find a representative from their project to join instead.
3. There may be a chance to offer volunteers extra credit / points in exchange for their efforts. Confirm this with HAAG leadership and course staff.
4. Ensure each project has a representative (some may not have managers).
5. Introduce the Open House idea in the channel and share the expectations with the team:
    -  Each project should optionally prepare a 10 minute presentation / demo of research activities
    -  Answer live questions from attendees, collect any questions which cannot be immediately answered
6. Provide template for presentation.

## Set Open House Date and Create Teams Meeting
1. Choose a date late in the semester, before classes end. Try to avoid the exams week.
2. The date should also be before the application deadline for research, generally set by Dr. Lytle and/or Dr. Shi. Confirm this with HAAG Leadership.
3. In Teams, create a meeting/webinar for the date.
4. 2. **MAKE SURE RECORDING IS ENABLED.**
5. Setup breakout rooms, one per participating project, and assign the represetative as an admin for the room.
6. Allow participants to join the rooms freely.
7. Enable recording for the session.
8. Agenda for the meeting will be something like:
    - 10 minutes of HAAG intro
    - Breakout sessions 45 minutes (5 minutes presentation, 20 minutes break for Q&A, 5 minutes presentation, 15 minutes final Q&A).
    - 5 minutes Farewell / Wrap-up (ask prospective researchers to take the survey).
9. Create a shareable meeting link.
10. Recommended to create a trial meeting first to make sure settings, etc. are all configured correctly.

## Draft and Send Email for the Event
1. Create an [email](https://github.com/Human-Augment-Analytics/Admin-Recruitment-Unit/blob/main/Open-House/email-templates.md#email-template-for-open-house-announcement) to send to a public mailing list or to other groups. Additionally craft a message to be shared in the HAAG org to be passed along to others.
2. The email/message should contain:
    - A brief intro to HAAG as a concept.
    - A list of the active projects.
    - The courses and roles associated with the program.
    - A link to the Teams event. (and maybe an attached .ics file of the event)
    - Who to contact for any questions about the event (likely you).
3. Get HAAG leadership to approve the messages as it is external facing, and email or share to the appropriate lists and groups.

## Run the Event
1. Follow the agenda laid out for the event.
2. **MAKE SURE RECORDING IS ENABLED.**
3. Explain to the attendees how the breakout rooms are going to work.
4. For the 10 mintute intro, optionally prepare a slideshow or show the website.
5. Note key individuals such as HAAG leadership.
6. Quickly list off the projects.
7. Start the breakout room session. Stay in the main room to answer any questions about technical issues.
8. For users on mobile, you may need to manually assign them to breakout rooms. Announce/ask them to put in the chat which room they need to enter.
9. Advise the representative teams to reach out via Slack if there are any issues.
10. During the Farewell / Wrap-up portion, thank everyone for attending and encourage them to fill out the quick survey.

## Follow-up - Post Video and Send Out
1. Following the meeting, once recordings are ready, upload them to the HAAG video platform (whatever exists, YouTube, etc.)
    - There should be a recording for the main event and one per room. You can decide to stitch these together into one long video and provide chapters / timestamps to link to each portion. This might be too long of a video however.
    - The other option (probably better option) would be to upload each video separately, add them all to a playlist, and then share a link to the playlist.
2. Credentials or permissions for upload to the platform may need to be obtained first, confirm this with HAAG Leadership. 
3. Draft a final [email](https://github.com/Human-Augment-Analytics/Admin-Recruitment-Unit/blob/main/Open-House/email-templates.md#email-template-for-prospective-researcher-survey) to attendees of the meeting thanking them and sharing the video recordings.
4. Include a link to the [survey](https://github.com/Human-Augment-Analytics/Admin-Recruitment-Unit/blob/main/Open-House/email-templates.md#email-template-for-prospective-researcher-survey) in the email and encourage them to take it.
5. Collect the results and share them with the HAAG leadership.
