# SOP 3.1 – Onboarding Packet

## Overview
This procedure ensures all new recruits receive consistent onboarding information to reduce confusion and accelerate contribution.

---

## Purpose
Standardize onboarding across projects to improve clarity, efficiency, and early productivity.

---

## Intended Audience
- New Research Recruits  
- Project Managers / Team Leads  

---

## When to Use
- Immediately after candidate acceptance  

---

## Procedure

### 1. Prepare Onboarding Packet
Create a centralized document including:

#### Required Sections
1. Project Overview  
2. Team Structure  
3. Tools & Access  
4. Workflow Guidelines  
5. Documentation Standards  
6. Expectations  

---

### 2. Distribute Packet
- Share in `#project-onboarding` channel  
- Tag new recruits  

---

### 3. Confirm Access
Ensure recruits have:
- Slack access  
- GitHub permissions  
- Required tools installed  

---

### 4. Conduct Onboarding Check-In
- Schedule onboarding meeting (optional)  
- Answer questions  

---

### 5. Validate Understanding
- Confirm recruits reviewed materials  
- Confirm understanding of expectations  

---

## Output
- Fully onboarded recruit  

---

## Success Criteria
- Reduced onboarding confusion  
- Faster time to first contribution  

---

## Enforcement
- No work begins before onboarding completion  
- Packet must be updated each semester  

---
