# Waitlist Email Procedure

## Intended Audience
Recruitment leads responsible for sending waitlist notifications.

---

## Purpose
To clearly communicate waitlist status and expected next steps to applicants.

---

## Procedure

1. Copy the waitlist email template below.
2. Replace all bracketed fields with applicant-specific information.
3. Confirm:
   - Waitlist status is clearly stated
   - Timeline or expectations are included (if available)
4. Send the email to the applicant.

---

## Email Template

Subject: HAAG Application Update

Dear [Applicant Name],

Thank you for your application to HAAG.

We would like to inform you that you have been placed on our waitlist. This means that we are still considering your application and may reach out if space becomes available.

We will provide an update by [Estimated Timeline].

Thank you for your patience, and please feel free to reach out with any questions.

Best regards,  
[Your Name]  
HAAG Recruitment Team

---

## Procedure Evaluation

**Clarity of Audience**  
The procedure is intended for recruitment leads managing waitlist communication.

**Appropriate Level of Detail**  
The steps focus on clarity and completeness without unnecessary complexity.

**Reproducibility**  
The template ensures consistent messaging and allows the process to be reused easily.

**Realism of Effort**  
Effort is minimal, requiring only template customization.

**Integration & Enforcement**  
The procedure can be integrated by storing templates centrally and encouraging their use during recruitment.
