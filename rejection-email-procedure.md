# Rejection Email Procedure

## Intended Audience
Recruitment leads responsible for sending rejection emails to applicants.

---

## Purpose
To communicate rejection decisions clearly and professionally while maintaining a respectful tone.

---

## Procedure

1. Copy the rejection email template below.
2. Replace all bracketed fields with applicant-specific information.
3. Confirm:
   - The rejection decision is clearly stated
   - The tone is professional and respectful
4. Send the email to the applicant.

---

## Email Template

Subject: HAAG Application Update

Dear [Applicant Name],

Thank you for your interest in HAAG and for taking the time to apply.

After careful consideration, we regret to inform you that we are unable to offer you a position at this time.

We appreciate your interest and encourage you to apply again in the future.

Best regards,  
[Your Name]  
HAAG Recruitment Team

---

## Procedure Evaluation

**Clarity of Audience**  
The procedure is clearly intended for recruitment leads managing applicant communication.

**Appropriate Level of Detail**  
The steps are simple and focused, appropriate for users who only need guidance on structure and tone.

**Reproducibility**  
The inclusion of a complete template ensures the process can be repeated consistently.

**Realism of Effort**  
Minimal effort is required, as users only need to personalize and send the template.

**Integration & Enforcement**  
Templates can be stored in a shared repository and used as a standard baseline, encouraging consistent usage.
