## Initiative Scope

This initiative focused on creating a standardized recruitment email communication framework for HAAG. Previously, recruitment emails (offers, rejections, waitlists, and reminders) were written independently by recruitment leads, leading to inconsistencies in tone, clarity, and structure.

The scope of this work included developing a set of reusable email templates and a basic procedure for how they should be used. The goal was to improve clarity for applicants and create a more consistent communication process across recruitment cycles.

---

## Alignment with HAAG’s Goals

This initiative aligns with HAAG’s goals of maintaining professionalism and improving clarity in communication.

Evidence of alignment is based on need for improvement of prior recruitment processes, where inconsistencies in messaging and structure were present. This initiative directly addresses those issues by introducing standardized templates.

Evaluation was conducted through cognitive walkthroughs, where the templates were reviewed from the perspective of an applicant to assess clarity of decisions, required actions, deadlines, and next steps. These walkthroughs indicated that the templates improved clarity and consistency.

## Procedures

### Overview

The procedure developed outlines how recruitment leads can use standardized email templates during the recruitment process.

Templates were created for:
- Offer emails
- Rejection emails  
- Waitlist emails  
- Reminder emails  

**Purpose of Procedures**  
To ensure consistent and clear communication with applicants while reducing the need to draft emails from scratch.
